# 00. Brief: what we are being judged on

## The challenge (verbatim essentials, from Resources/Hackathon Challenge.pdf)
"Reimagine UFCU's New Member Onboarding Experience." Build a prototype that helps a new member join
UFCU quickly and securely and use one or more products. UFCU explicitly says: do not build real identity
verification, real banking integrations, or production systems. "Mock, simulate, or invent what you need."
"A great concept prototype beats a half-finished system."

Suggested exploration areas: establishing identity in a member-centric UFCU way; collecting required
information safely; applying for Everyday Banking, Mortgage, Consumer Lending, or Business Banking
products; enhancing features that support the member's needs.

## What we must show at 3:00 PM
1. A code-based prototype that highlights the onboarding workflow (must run locally on a laptop).
2. A brief explanation of how the solution improves onboarding.
3. How the credit union can trust the new member's identity.
4. How a user gets from interest to membership-ready.
5. How trust and identity confidence are established without breaking the member experience.

Plus the "Participant Expectations" sheet: a design prototype (Figma or equivalent), a working web app,
and a live 5-minute demo with 2 minutes of judge Q&A. Slides optional.

"What good looks like": **simple**, and **accessible to all generations** (boomer through alpha).

## Judging (official scorecard, received at kickoff)
100 points. A. Member onboarding and product fit 25; B. Speed and friction reduction 20; C. UX
intuitiveness and creativity 20; D. Technical execution and feasibility 15; E. Compliance, trust and
risk awareness 10; F. Pitch and communication 10. Each rated 1 to 5 and weighted. Full mapping of what
earns a 5 to what we build is in docs/09-rubric-map.md. Deliverables are due 2:30 PM, not 3:00.

## Definition of a member
Anyone with a deposit account (savings, checking, certificate), a loan (credit card, auto, line of credit),
or a mortgage. Savings is the membership share account; everything else attaches to it.
