## 1. Membership eligibility

UFCU's account-opening page states eligibility requires being "an employee, student or alumni of" one of the following organizations (source: https://ufcu.org/open-account):

- Ascension/Seton
- Austin Community College (ACC)
- Concordia University (CTX)
- Foundation Communities
- Galveston College
- Goodwill
- Huston-Tillotson University (HT)
- Indeed
- St. Edward's University (SEU)
- Southwestern University (SU)
- Temple College (TC)
- Texas A&M at Galveston (TAMUG)
- University of Texas at Austin (UT Austin)
- University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB)
- UTHealth-Houston
- YMCA

(source: https://ufcu.org/open-account)

Alternative path: anyone can "join UFCU through the American Consumer Council for free" -- this appears to be a blanket eligibility path that UFCU handles as part of enrollment, with no extra action described (source: https://ufcu.org/open-account).

The general About page uses broader, non-specific language: "Maybe you're not a Member yet, but you can be. We're confident you're eligible to join and it's easy to apply. We eagerly serve every individual Member and the larger communities in which we live, work, and play." It also states UFCU is non-discriminatory "with respect to whom we serve on all grounds, including but not limited to race, color, religion, creed, gender, gender expression, age, national origin (ancestry), mental or physical disability, marital status, sexual orientation, military status, or political affiliation" (source: https://ufcu.org/about).

Gaps: no explicit list of counties served, no explicit SEG (Select Employee Group) employer list beyond the organizations above, and no explicit statement about joining via a family member who is already a member. See Gaps section.

## 2. Deposit products

### Checking accounts (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/overview unless noted)

| Account | Opening deposit | Monthly fee | Fee waiver / notes | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teen Checking (ages 13-17) | $0 | $0 | Requires a parent/guardian/other adult as joint owner; converts to Free Checking at 18 (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/teen) | "Monthly dividends up to 0.50% APY" for balances under $500; 0.00% APY at $500+, effective October 1, 2021 (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/teen) |
| Simply U(TM) | $0 | $0 | "Hassle-free basic banking," no minimum/maximum balance; described by UFCU as for "any Member who wants all the basics, without the worry of overspending or monthly fees"; a parent/guardian must be on the account for anyone under 18 (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/simply-u/detail) | No dividends |
| Free Checking | $0 | $0 | Includes "$400 Courtesy Pay" | No dividends |
| Plus Checking | $0 | $10/month | Waived with combined month-end balances of $10,000+ OR monthly qualifying electronic deposits of $4,000+. Effective October 1, 2024. | "Bonus Dividends up to 2.25% APY" on balances up to $10,000; includes "$1,000 Courtesy Pay," 0.25% auto loan rate discount, $200 mortgage fee credit, international transaction fee reimbursement |

Teen Checking details: ATM cash withdrawal limit of $500/day; only an SSN and a guardian are needed to open at a branch; instant-issue contactless Visa debit card available same day (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/teen).

Simply U card details: $1,000 daily ATM cash withdrawal limit, $2,000 maximum daily purchases, usable internationally with a 1% international service fee, no overdraft fees (account can show a temporary negative balance if final merchant charges exceed the authorization hold) (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/simply-u/detail).

No dedicated student checking account distinct from Simply U/Free Checking was found; UFCU's college-student-facing content focuses on credit cards, not a named "student checking" product (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2024/10/21/credit-cards-college-students).

### Business deposit products

Business Checking: no monthly service fee, no minimum balance, 50 free "account events" (deposits/withdrawals) per month, $0.10 per additional event; NCUA insured to $250,000 (source: https://ufcu.org/business/checking/checking). Note: the business fee schedule separately lists a "Premium Business Checking" tier with a $20.00/month service fee and the same "first 50 free, then $0.10/item" analysis-fee structure (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/tools/business-rates-and-fees) -- there appear to be at least two business checking tiers (a free one and a "Premium" one), though the free tier's page did not itself use the word "Premium."

Business Savings Account: fee-free, no monthly charge, 0.010% APY effective October 1, 2021, dividends calculated daily/posted quarterly on balances of $100+, NCUA insured to $250,000 (source: https://ufcu.org/business/savings/savings).

Premium (Business) Savings Account: fee-free, 0.250% APY effective October 1, 2021, same features as Business Savings otherwise (source: https://ufcu.org/business/savings/savings).

Business Money Market and Business Certificates are referenced in site navigation/product menus but no rate or minimum-deposit detail was found on the pages fetched (source: https://ufcu.org/business/savings/savings).

## 3. Credit cards, personal loans, auto loans, student loans, mortgages

### Credit cards (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/credit-cards/compare)

- **Great Rate Card** -- low-rate card, "rates as low as 9.49% APR," $0 balance-transfer fee, $0 annual fee. Eligibility: must maintain a UFCU checking account with a $500+ balance, a $50+ monthly balance for 3 months, or active direct deposits; subject to credit approval.
- **Cash Back Card** -- "1.5% cash back earned per $1 spent," $0 annual fee, "rates as low as 14.90% APR." Same checking-relationship eligibility as above; credit approval required.
- **Travel & Rewards Card** -- "2 points earned per $1 spent," $0 annual fee, "rates as low as 13.90% APR." Standard account and credit-approval requirements apply.

All cards include fraud protection, zero liability on unauthorized purchases, contactless payment, and card lock/unlock in the app (source: same page).

Student-specific card note: UFCU's own article states "student and college credit cards are designed for beginners and often don't require a credit history to qualify," but does not name a distinct branded "student card" separate from the three above, and does not address international-student or no-SSN eligibility (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2024/10/21/credit-cards-college-students).

### Personal loans (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/personal)

- **Signature (unsecured) Loan**: APR range 12.90% (excellent credit) to 17.90% (fair/marginal/low-no score); example payment "$22.70 per $1,000 borrowed"; terms up to 60 months.
- **Personal Line of Credit**: same 12.90%-17.90% APR tiering; interest charged only on amount drawn; functions with overdraft-protection use case; replenishes as paid down.
- **Share- or certificate-secured loans**: lower rates available; the pledged savings/certificate continues earning dividends while used as collateral.

"No hidden fees" is advertised; specific eligibility beyond "subject to credit evaluation... certain restrictions apply" is not detailed (source: same page).

### Credit Builder Loan (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/credit-builder)

- Loan amount: $500-$2,500.
- Rate: 3.00% APR for all terms.
- Term: 6-24 months, borrower's choice.
- Minimum monthly payment: at least $30/month, borrower selects amount.
- No application fee.
- Mechanism: UFCU holds the loan proceeds; on-time payments are reported to the three major credit bureaus; borrower receives the accumulated funds after the final payment.
- Designed for members with little to no credit or poor credit history; "subject to credit eligibility. Certain restrictions apply" (exact qualification criteria not published).

### Auto loans (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/auto)

Rates "as low as 4.99% APR," tiered by credit score and term:

| Term | Excellent | Good | Average | Fair | Marginal | Low/No Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-48 mo | 4.99% | 6.24% | 7.49% | 9.39% | 14.65% | 17.25% |
| 49-66 mo | 5.64% | 6.64% | 7.89% | 9.64% | 15.10% | 17.70% |
| 67+ mo | 5.89% | 6.89% | 8.14% (7.89% for vehicles <10 yrs/<100k mi) | 9.89% (9.64% for vehicles <10 yrs/<100k mi) | 15.35% | 17.90% |

Finances new vehicles, used vehicles, and refinances of existing auto loans. Loans of $100,000+ require a phone call to (512) 498-CARS (2277). No first-time-buyer or student-specific discount was found on this page (source: same page). Note: Plus Checking includes a 0.25% auto loan rate discount (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/overview).

### Student loans

No dedicated UFCU-originated student loan product page was found. UFCU's "investments" microsite (investments-cfs.ufcu.org, a separate advisory-services domain) publishes educational articles about managing existing federal/private student loan debt (income-driven repayment, refinancing tips), not a UFCU student-loan origination product (source: WebSearch results for site:ufcu.org student loans, e.g. https://investments-cfs.ufcu.org/resource-center/money/strategies-for-managing-student-loan-debt and https://www.ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2024/10/21/three-steps-to-less-college-debt). Treat "UFCU offers student loans" as unverified.

### Mortgages / home loans (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/home)

The loans hub page describes "Home Loans" as: mortgage solutions with options "as low as 5% down payment," including conventional, FHA, VA, and specialty programs. "Subject to credit eligibility; certain restrictions apply." Plus Checking includes a $200 mortgage fee credit (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/overview). A dedicated deep-dive mortgage page was not found at guessed URLs (`/personal/loans/mortgage`, `/personal/loans/home/mortgages` both 404'd); rate detail, jumbo-loan terms, and first-time-homebuyer program specifics were not retrievable from pages fetched.

### Other loans

- Motorcycle, RV & Boat Loans: specialized recreational-vehicle/watercraft financing; no rate/eligibility detail retrieved (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/home, linking to /personal/loans/rv-boat).
- Business loans: Auto, Equipment, Line of Credit, and Working Capital loans for businesses require: business operating for at least 2 years, applicant an owner for at least 2 years, applicant owns at least 25% of the business, business permitted to do business in Texas, business located in Texas. Business Loan Documentation Fee: $175.00 (source: https://ufcu.org/business/business-requirements and https://ufcu.org/resources/tools/business-rates-and-fees).

## 4. Business banking / business accounts

Products: Business Checking (see section 2), a "Premium Business Checking" fee tier ($20/month per the business fee schedule), Business Savings, Premium (Business) Savings, and Business Money Market / Business Certificates referenced in navigation without further detail retrieved (source: https://ufcu.org/business/checking/checking, https://ufcu.org/business/savings/savings, https://ufcu.org/resources/tools/business-rates-and-fees).

### Documents required to open a UFCU business account, by entity type

(source: https://ufcu.org/docs/default-source/instructions/docs-required-to-open-a-ufcu-business-account.pdf, dated 04/22 on the document itself)

| Entity type | Required documents |
|---|---|
| Sole Proprietorship | Assumed Name Certificate (DBA) if owner's first/last name is not in the business name; SSN or EIN |
| Limited Liability Company (LLC) | Certificate of Formation filed with the Secretary of State; EIN (single-member LLC may use SSN instead of EIN) |
| Professional Limited Liability Company (PLLC) | Certificate of Formation filed with the Secretary of State; proof of professional licensing in the State of Texas; EIN (single-member PLLC may use SSN instead of EIN) |
| Corporation (for-profit and not-for-profit) | Certificate of Formation filed with the Secretary of State; EIN |
| General Partnership | Partnership Agreement (naming the business and partners); Assumed Name Certificate (DBA); EIN |
| Limited Partnership (LP) | Certificate of Formation filed with the Secretary of State; EIN |
| Unincorporated Associations/Organization | Organizing documents (e.g. bylaws, meeting minutes, charter); EIN |

Beneficial Ownership Rule (effective May 11, 2018, per Bank Secrecy Act / FinCEN requirements, applies to LLCs, corporations, partnerships, and similar entities filed with a Secretary of State -- does NOT apply to sole proprietorships or unincorporated associations): the entity must identify and provide name, address, date of birth, SSN, and valid government-issued ID for (i) any individual who directly or indirectly owns 25%+ equity in the business, AND (ii) one individual with significant responsibility to control/manage/direct the entity (e.g. CEO, COO, CFO, Managing Member, General Partner, President, VP, Treasurer). UFCU may also request a copy of a driver's license or similar ID for each listed beneficial owner (source: same PDF).

## 5. Account opening requirements

Per the account-opening page (source: https://ufcu.org/open-account):

- Valid driver license or state ID required.
- Social Security Number **or ITIN** accepted (page explicitly allows SSN/ITIN).
- Must be 18 years of age or older to open a standard account; if requirements/documents aren't met, UFCU directs applicants to "schedule an appointment at your local branch" (implying case-by-case exceptions are handled in person, not online).
- Application described as a "3-5 minute process" online.
- Support contacts: (512) 467-8080 or (800) 252-8311; branch appointment scheduling at https://appointments.ufcu.org/service.

Minors: Teen Checking (ages 13-17) requires "additional ownership by a parent, guardian, or other adult" -- i.e., a joint/parent owner is mandatory for under-18 accounts; only an SSN and a guardian are needed at a branch (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/teen). Simply U also states "a parent or guardian will need to be on the account for anyone under 18 years of age" (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/simply-u/detail).

Non-US citizens / international students: No page fetched contained explicit guidance on ITIN vs. SSN procedures for international students, nor any mention of passport, I-20, or visa document acceptance. The only relevant data point is that the general account-opening page lists "Social Security Number/ITIN" as an accepted identifier, which implies ITIN holders (a category that commonly includes some international students and nonresident taxpayers) can open at least some accounts, but UFCU's website does not spell out an international-student-specific policy, required visa/I-20 documentation, or whether a US-issued ID is mandatory in addition to a passport. This is a confirmed gap -- see Gaps section; do not assert an international-student policy beyond what is written here.

Minimum opening deposit: $0 for Teen Checking, Simply U, Free Checking, and Plus Checking (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/overview); $1 for regular Savings; $2,500 for Personal Money Market; $1,000 for Certificates; $100-$1,000 for IRAs (source: https://ufcu.org/personal/savings/overview). No page specified a minimum "membership share" deposit distinct from these product minimums.

Joint owners: supported generally (referenced under Account Management FAQs, which cover "adding joint account holders, beneficiaries, and Powers of Attorney" -- source: https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/account-management); mandatory for minors as noted above.

Funding options for opening: not detailed on any page fetched (e.g., whether ACH pull, debit card funding, or cash/check-only at a branch is supported online). Gap.

## 6. Digital banking features

- **Mobile app**: live features include mobile check deposit; card controls (activate, lock/unlock, replacement cards, set/change PIN, credit card spend limits); "Member to Member Transfers" for scheduling immediate transfers to other UFCU members; personal finance management/budgeting and savings tracking are listed as "coming next" (not yet live as of the fetched page). Requires iOS 16.0+ or Android 12.0+; available on the App Store and Google Play (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/member-services/banking/mobile).
- **Zelle**: UFCU "proudly partners with Zelle" for person-to-person payments; the specific enrollment steps and transfer limits were not present in the fetched page content (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/member-services/banking/zelle). Gap -- limits not verified.
- **ATM network**: UFCU advertises "55,000+ fee-free ATMs" across: UFCU's own ATM network; the Allpoint Network (found at retailers like CVS, Walgreens, and Target, with select locations accepting cash deposits); the Austin ATM Alliance (a coalition of Austin-area credit unions offering surcharge-free access); and the Gulf Coast Chapter ATM Alliance (15+ Texas locations for Gulf Coast credit union members). No mention of CO-OP network participation was found, and no campus-specific (UT) ATM locations were called out by name on the fetched page (source: https://ufcu.org/locations/fee-free-atms). Non-UFCU/non-Allpoint ATM transactions cost $1.00 per the fee schedule (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/tools/rates-fees).
- **Branches near UT Austin campus**:
  - University Branch -- 2244 Guadalupe St, Austin, TX 78705-5217; phone (512) 467-8080 / (800) 252-8311; lobby Mon-Fri 9am-5pm, closed Saturday; located in West Campus/"The Drag" adjacent to UT; has enhanced ATMs including a walk-up deposit-accepting ATM and an on-site LPL Financial Advisor (source: https://ufcu.org/locations/details/university).
  - N Guadalupe Branch -- 4611 Guadalupe St, Austin, TX 78751-2908; phone (512) 467-8080; lobby Mon-Fri 9am-5pm (drive-thru to 6pm), Saturday lobby 10am-2pm (drive-thru 9am-2pm) (source: https://www.ufcu.org/locations/n-guadalupe).
  - General Member Services phone hours: Mon-Fri 8am-5:30pm, Sat 10am-2pm (source: https://ufcu.org/locations).

## 8. Student / international student / new-to-Texas / small-business / first-time-account-holder specifics

- **Students**: UT Austin (and several other Austin-area schools: ACC, Concordia, Huston-Tillotson, St. Edward's, Southwestern) students/employees/alumni are explicitly eligible for membership (source: https://ufcu.org/open-account). UFCU publishes a "Best Credit Cards for College Students" article stating student/college credit cards "don't require a credit history to qualify" (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2024/10/21/credit-cards-college-students). Teen Checking (13-17) and Simply U are the low/no-fee accounts most relevant to young or first-time account holders (sources: https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/teen, https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/simply-u/detail).
- **International students**: No dedicated page or FAQ addressing international-student account opening, ITIN-specific procedures, or acceptance of a passport/I-20/visa in lieu of a US driver's license/state ID was found across the open-account page, the general FAQs, the Account Management FAQs, or the Simply U FAQ. The only supporting fact is that "Social Security Number/ITIN" is listed as an acceptable identifier on the general account-opening page (source: https://ufcu.org/open-account). This is a material gap for an onboarding chatbot serving international students -- flag to the user rather than inventing a policy.
- **New to Texas**: no dedicated content found addressing new-to-Texas residents specifically (e.g., no out-of-state ID or address-verification workaround was described). Gap.
- **Small business owners**: covered in Section 4 above; UFCU's Business Requirements page and the "Requirements to Open a UFCU Business Account" PDF are the authoritative sources for documentation by entity type (source: https://ufcu.org/business/business-requirements, https://ufcu.org/docs/default-source/instructions/docs-required-to-open-a-ufcu-business-account.pdf). Business credit/loan eligibility additionally requires 2+ years in business, 2+ years of ownership tenure, 25%+ ownership stake, and Texas-based/Texas-permitted operation (source: https://ufcu.org/business/business-requirements).
- **First-time account holders generally**: Simply U is explicitly framed as the account "for any Member who wants all the basics, without the worry of overspending or monthly fees," with no minimum/maximum balance requirement and no overdraft fees (source: https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/simply-u/detail), making it the most likely first product to recommend to a first-time account holder in an onboarding flow, though UFCU's own copy does not use the phrase "first-time account holder."
