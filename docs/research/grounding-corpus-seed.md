## https://ufcu.org/personal/checking/simply-u

Simply U is UFCU's entry-level checking account, built around a "spend what you deposit" model so overdrafts cannot occur. It costs $0 to open, with no minimum opening deposit. There is no monthly service fee, no overdraft fee, and no deposit fee; bill pay is free. The account comes with an instant-issue, contactless Visa debit card and does not support check-writing. ATM cash withdrawals are capped at $1,000 per day, and the account has access to over 500 fee-free ATMs nationwide. Standard features include online banking, mobile app access, mobile check deposit, and direct deposit setup. Visa Zero Liability fraud protection applies to unauthorized card use. The page states members "must meet eligibility requirements" but does not enumerate what those requirements are on-page (not verified: whether age, residency, or student status factor into eligibility). Because it removes overdraft risk and has no minimum deposit or monthly fee, this is the most likely first account for a student, international student, or anyone new to banking in the US and wanting a simple, low-risk starting product.

## https://ufcu.org/personal/loans/credit-builder

The Credit Builder Loan is designed for members with little, no, or poor credit history. UFCU holds a designated loan amount while the member makes monthly payments toward it; each month's payment is reported to the three major credit bureaus. Once all payments are complete, the member receives the accumulated funds. Loan amounts range from $500 to $2,500, with member-selected terms of 6 to 24 months. The minimum monthly payment is $30. The interest rate is a flat 3.00% APR across all amounts and terms, and there is no application fee. The page explicitly says not to worry about having little to no credit, or even poor credit, when applying. This product is a direct, named answer to "how do I build credit as a student or newcomer with no US credit history."

## https://ufcu.org/resources/member-services/banking/mobile

The UFCU Mobile app requires iOS 16.0+ or Android 12.0+ and is downloaded from the App Store or Google Play. First-time users complete an enrollment process using their account number plus identifying information such as SSN or date of birth. Current features include mobile check deposit, bill pay and transfers (to both UFCU members and non-members), card management (activation, locking, PIN changes), account alerts, transaction history, credit score monitoring, statement access, mortgage payment processing, and credit card cash advances/spend limits. Planned/upcoming features include personal finance management, expense tracking, and savings goal setting (not yet live as of this page). The app has daily maintenance windows from 10 PM to 1 AM, and a longer Sunday window from midnight to 4 AM, during which transfers, deposits, alerts, and card features may be delayed or unavailable. Security is described as including a "universal password across all accounts" shared with online banking, plus SSL encryption. This is the primary reference for "how do I set up and use the UFCU app."

## https://ufcu.org/resources/member-services/banking/digital-payment-solutions

UFCU supports adding a UFCU debit or credit card to Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, and Garmin Pay digital wallets, done through the UFCU Mobile app "with the tap of a button." UFCU also supports PayPal and Zelle as payment services. For online or in-app purchases, members select their preferred digital wallet and verify information; for in-store purchases, they locate the contactless symbol at checkout and hold their phone near the reader. If a card needs activation first, members can call 1-866-928-5450 or activate through online banking or the mobile app. Security is described as using biometric technology and tokenization, and the page states the actual card number is never shared with the merchant during a digital wallet transaction. No fees for these services are mentioned on the page. This page is the best available source on digital wallets and third-party payment apps, though it does not give Zelle-specific transaction limits or enrollment steps (see the dedicated Zelle page note below).

## https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/digital-banking

This is a long FAQ covering online and mobile banking mechanics. Enrollment can be done via browser or the mobile app; one shared password works across Online Banking and the Mobile app. Password rules: 8-40 characters, must include upper and lower case letters plus at least one number, and cannot contain the member's name, SSN, or consecutive characters. One-time passcodes are valid for 10 minutes and are single-use; a device/browser can be marked "trusted" for 30 days to skip repeated passcodes. Roughly one year of transaction history is viewable online, and up to 84 months of statements are retrievable. "Transfer to a Non-UFCU Member" lets a member send funds to any US email address or phone number with no UFCU fee; UFCU-to-UFCU and debit-card transfers are instant (debit transfer carries a $1 fee), ACH-based transfers take up to 2 business days, and unclaimed transfers are automatically canceled after 7 days with no debit to the sender. Mobile deposit has no stated fee and requires endorsing the check "For Mobile Deposit Only"; physical checks should be kept at least 10 days after deposit before destroying. Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity with a 60-second warning.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/overdraft-services

UFCU defines an overdraft as when an account lacks funds to cover a transaction but the transaction is allowed anyway. Two overdraft tools are described. Overdraft Protection Transfer automatically pulls funds from up to 3 linked accounts (savings, Money Market, line of credit, or another checking account) in a member-set priority order, with no fee for the transfers themselves; enrollment is available online (for savings/Money Market), by phone, or in a branch. Courtesy Pay is a separate, opt-in service (opt-in required specifically for everyday debit card signature transactions; checks and automatic payments are covered without opt-in). Courtesy Pay limits are $100 for new accounts, then after 90 days: $400 for Free/Teen Checking, $1,000 for Plus Checking, and $1,500 for Business accounts. There is no fee for an overdraft under $5; overdrafts of $5 or more carry a per-item fee, and members have 45 days to repay the overdrawn amount plus fees. A Line of Credit (starting at $500, variable rate based on creditworthiness) is also offered as an overdraft cushion, charging interest only on the amount actually borrowed.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/security

This FAQ distinguishes a scam (a suspicious message or request, not yet acted on) from fraud (an activity that has already occurred). It repeatedly states that UFCU will never ask for a password, PIN, or one-time passcode by phone, text, or email, and that requests to move money to a "safe account" are a common scam tactic. Members are told to pause and independently verify any urgent or unexpected request rather than responding directly. For unauthorized card or account activity, members should call (866) 928-5450 to start a dispute or fraud claim. To report a scam or suspicious activity more generally, the page gives fraud@ufcu.org or a phone menu path: (512) 467-8080, then Option 5, then Option 4, then Option 2. Notably, the page repeats the warning found elsewhere that peer-to-peer transfers such as Zelle "may be treated like cash and are not always reversible," so members should be cautious sending money to anyone they have not verified independently.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/cards

This FAQ (48 items) covers both credit and debit cards. For lost, stolen, or fraudulent credit cards, the number given is (800) 252-8311; for Card Keeper-related lost/stolen reporting, (888) 842-6328 is given as the immediate-action number. Credit card disputes must be submitted in writing within 60 days to a stated PO Box address. Adding an authorized user takes 2-3 business days to process, with a new card arriving in 7-10 business days. Card Keeper is UFCU's self-service card-control feature (in online banking or the mobile app) allowing card lock/unlock, PIN changes, and replacement requests. On debit cards, preauthorization holds typically last about 72 hours (gas station holds can be as high as $75 and typically release after 2 hours); UFCU recommends using a credit card, or a debit card with PIN entry, at gas stations and hotels to avoid larger holds. Contactless "tap to pay" chip cards use NFC and generate a unique one-time code per transaction so the card number itself is not transmitted, meaning skimming devices cannot compromise a tap-to-pay transaction.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/faqs/account-management

This FAQ focuses on account access arrangements. UFCU advises never sharing a password, even with someone trusted, and instead recommends adding a joint account holder, beneficiary, or Power of Attorney (POA), each with different access. A joint account holder has full account access, including their own debit card and unique login, and shares responsibility for fees or negative balances; they also have the same Bill Pay and transfer permissions as the primary owner. A POA can manage finances (pay bills, withdraw funds) while the primary member is alive, but that authority ends at the member's death. A beneficiary has no access during the member's lifetime and can only claim remaining funds after presenting a death certificate. These changes cannot be completed online; they require a branch visit or a call to Member Services. This is useful if onboarding covers a parent/guardian helping a student set up or co-manage an account.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2026/08/19/what-is-a-credit-union

This article explains that a credit union is a not-for-profit financial cooperative owned by the people it serves, contrasted with a bank owned by outside shareholders. Members elect volunteer board directors and each member has equal voting rights regardless of account balance. Earnings are described as being used to strengthen the credit union, improve services, fund education, or create value for members rather than paying outside shareholders. Benefits highlighted include competitive loan rates, fewer fees, savings dividends, and NCUA federal deposit insurance protection up to $250,000. Limitations noted include membership eligibility restrictions, fewer physical branch locations than large banks compared to some competitors, and that loan approval still depends on individual credit factors. The article states UFCU itself was founded in 1936 and serves Texas communities with Austin-area branches, online/mobile access, and access to over 55,000 fee-free ATMs. This is the best available page for explaining "what is a credit union and why would I join one" to someone completely new to the concept.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/articles/detail/articles/2024/10/21/finance-tips-for-college-grads

This article gives four financial priorities for recent college graduates. First, secure employment, noting that student loan repayment typically begins about six months after graduation. Second, prioritize retirement savings early to take advantage of compound interest, especially if an employer offers a matching contribution. Third, understand your credit score, since a weak score raises interest rates on major purchases and can cause loan denials; the article notes UFCU offers free credit reviews to help members understand their score. Fourth, build a specific student loan repayment strategy rather than only paying the minimum, noting that most lenders offer multiple repayment options including possible consolidation or forgiveness programs. While aimed at grads rather than incoming students, this article is a close proxy for "how should I think about money as a young adult" content relevant to the onboarding persona.

## https://ufcu.org/resources/tools/member-relief-center

This page describes UFCU's hardship-support options. Member Relief Loans are low-interest loans for qualifying members, with no payment required for up to 90 days once approved (phone: 512-498-2277). Members facing hardship may also be able to skip payments on consumer loans and credit cards, or get mortgage-specific assistance for existing UFCU mortgages (mortgage line: 800-240-4214; consumer loans/cards: 800-252-8311). A separate track exists for businesses facing community-level hardship (512-493-0531 or 800-252-8311 option 5 then 1). The page also compiles external community resources: Findhelp.org (ZIP-code-based social services), 2-1-1 Texas (24/7 hotline, 877-541-7905), ConnectATX.org (multilingual Austin community resource connector), and the Central Texas Food Bank locator. This is useful lower-frequency but important safety-net content for a new member who runs into financial trouble after joining.
