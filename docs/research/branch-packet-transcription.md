## What matters for onboarding

- **Certification of Foreign Status (Form W-8BEN)** is a standard IRS form, not a UFCU-specific form. It certifies that an individual account applicant is NOT a U.S. person for tax purposes. It requires a foreign tax identifying number (line 6a) or an SSN/ITIN (line 5) if one is required; it does not "replace" an SSN outright, but the packet's own Membership and Account Application (page 2) treats "I am not a U.S. Citizen and agree to complete a W-8 or other applicable form" as an alternative to the standard SSN/backup-withholding certification, so a foreign national member can open an account by completing a W-8BEN instead of certifying a U.S. SSN/TIN. An onboarding assistant should flag that a member without an SSN needs to complete this form (and may still need an ITIN in some cases) rather than assuming SSN is always required.
- **The Membership and Account Application and Account Card** is the actual new-account/new-member intake form. It requires: full legal name, SSN, date of birth, physical home address (no P.O. boxes), a valid government ID (driver's license, state ID, military ID, U.S. passport, or permanent resident card with expiration date), and signatures. It also covers joint owners and optional Payable-on-Death (POD) beneficiaries, and includes a mandatory TIN/backup-withholding certification and a consensual lien / right-of-setoff disclosure (UFCU can apply a member's own deposits against amounts the member owes UFCU).
- **The debit/checking card sheet (UFCU_CARD, UFCU_FLYER)** advertises the "Simply U" account (aimed at students): free to open, no monthly/overdraft fees or minimum balance, instant-issue contactless VISA debit card, digital banking, and a sweepstakes tie-in ("Cash for College," $1,000 drawing for opening a checking account). This is UFCU's flagship low-friction student onboarding product.
- **Checking account tiers** (from the comparison table) are Plus, Free, Simply U, and Teen (13-17). Key distinguishing facts: only Plus earns dividends normally and offers Courtesy Pay up to $1,000 overdraft, reimbursed international transaction fees, bonus credit card rewards, auto loan rate discounts, and mortgage fee credits; Free and Teen offer $400 Courtesy Pay overdraft; Simply U has no Courtesy Pay/overdraft coverage at all. All four have $0 minimum to open.
- **UT campus ATMs**: about 14 ATMs exist on the UT Austin campus, but only some accept deposits. Confirmed deposit-capable ATMs: UT Student Services Building, Co-op East (2902 Medical Arts Street), UT Gregory Gym (also a full branch, open 10am-4pm Monday-Friday), UT Perry-Castaneda Library (21st & Speedway), and UT Robert Lee Moore Hall (Dean Keeton & Speedway). The UT Student Union ATM explicitly does NOT accept deposits. An onboarding assistant should not assume every campus ATM takes deposits.
- **The University Branch** (the full-service branch nearest UT campus) is at 2244 Guadalupe St, Austin, TX 78705/78712, phone (512) 467-8080, located on Guadalupe Street between The Co-op and Sweetgreen.
- **Wire transfer alternatives for international members**: UFCU has no international correspondent banking relationship, so it cannot directly receive international wires or wires in foreign currency; an international wire must route through a U.S. correspondent bank using UFCU's ABA routing number 314977405, and any foreign-currency wire gets converted to USD before reaching UFCU. For simpler/cheaper international transfers, branch staff informally point members (not a UFCU-endorsed recommendation) to Wise.com and Revolut.com as commonly used alternatives among UT students.
- **Official UFCU wire instructions**: Receiving Financial Institution = University Federal Credit Union, Austin, TX, ABA #314977405; beneficiary fields needed are the member's 13-digit account number (with 4-digit prefix: 1000, 1700, or 1800), member's name, and member's address, and the name/address given to the originating bank must match the UFCU account exactly.
- **Mortgage Services** covers standard products (FHA, VA, USDA, Conventional, Jumbo, Refinance, Investment, Second Homes) plus special programs: the Community Hero Loan Program (3% minimum down, fixed rate, no mortgage insurance, primary residence only) for a defined list of community-service professions including UT Austin staff, teachers, medical professionals, and first responders; HomeStart Down Payment Assistance (up to 5% of sales price, income limits apply, 680 minimum credit score, homebuyer education required); and a First Time Home Buyer Loan Program (100% financing, no monthly mortgage insurance, no income/location restrictions, can't have owned a home in the last three years).

Routing number is 314977405 for all UFCU accounts. UFCU was founded in 1936. UFCU is described elsewhere in the packet as "Austin's #1 Credit Union" for mortgages, and as the "Official Credit Union Sponsor of Texas Athletics."
