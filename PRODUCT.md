# PRODUCT.md

## Register
Mixed. The landing (`/`) is a brand surface that must read as a page on ufcu.org. The desk (`/desk`) and the Secure Zone (`/apply`) are product surfaces where design serves a task. Brand fidelity outranks novelty everywhere: the judge is UFCU's technology leadership and the bar is "we could ship this."

## What it is
Front Desk: UFCU's new-member onboarding reimagined for the Develop U hackathon (2026-09-19). A person says what they need in their language, the desk recommends accounts with reasons linked to ufcu.org, then hands off a prefilled application in a visibly separate secure page with identity paths that include no-SSN applicants.

## Users
Prospective members: UT students, international students without an SSN, people switching from a big bank, small business owners, retirees new to Austin. Ages 18 to 70. Many on phones. Several read Spanish, Korean, Portuguese, or French first.

## Brand personality (three physical words)
Welcoming (a staffed front desk, not a kiosk), plainspoken (Title Case headings, short sentences, "u" as a wink), sturdy (navy and orange, no gradients, no glass).

## References
ufcu.org itself: white header with the oval logo, Montserrat 700 headings in navy, Inter body in near-black, orange pill buttons, teal links, navy page-title bands, gray secondary nav band.

## Anti-references
ChatGPT-style chat wrappers. Fintech-neon dark mode. Cream-and-serif editorial. Any competitor's "enter your SSN" first screen.

## Strategic design principles
1. Order of operations is the product: information before identification.
2. Every recommendation carries its ufcu.org source.
3. The Secure Zone must look different at a glance, using UFCU's own navy, not a foreign theme.
4. Five languages are first-class, not a toggle.
5. Simulated parts are labeled.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, 16px minimum body, larger-text toggle, keyboard focus visible, reduced motion respected.
