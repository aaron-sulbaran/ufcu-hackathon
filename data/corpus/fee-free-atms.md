UFCU advertises more than 55,000 fee-free ATMs. They come from four networks: UFCU's own ATMs, the Allpoint Network found at retailers such as CVS, Walgreens, and Target with select locations accepting cash deposits, the Austin ATM Alliance of Austin-area credit unions, and the Gulf Coast Chapter ATM Alliance across 15 or more Texas locations. Using an ATM outside those networks costs $1.00 per transaction.

UFCU's own branch materials count more than 25 fee-free ATMs on and around the UT Austin campus, alongside 4 campus locations and 22 Austin-area locations.

The campus ATMs that accept deposits include the Student Services Building at West Dean Keeton and University Avenue, Co-op East at 2902 Medical Arts Street, Gregory Gym in front of the Shake Smart, and the Perry-Castaneda Library at 21st Street and Speedway. The Gregory Gym location is also a full branch, open 10 AM to 4 PM Monday through Friday. The ATM behind the Student Union, in the seating area of the building with Jamba Juice and Panda Express, does not take deposits.

If you live on or near campus, you can bank without ever paying an ATM fee.
