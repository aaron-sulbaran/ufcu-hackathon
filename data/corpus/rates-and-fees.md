Rates as published on the rates and fees page. Free Checking has no monthly fee. Plus Checking has a $10 monthly fee, waived at a $10,000 combined balance or $4,000 in monthly qualifying deposits, and pays a bonus dividend up to 2.25% APY. Basic Savings pays 0.01% APY and Teen savings 0.25% APY. Money Market is tiered from 0.01% to 3.25% APY by balance. Certificates and IRAs run 3.25% to 4.10% APY by term.

On the lending side, auto loan APRs run 4.99% to 17.90% depending on credit and term, and credit card APRs run 9.49% to 17.90%. The Credit Builder Loan is a flat 3.00% APR.

Fees worth knowing before you open: $1.00 for using an ATM outside the UFCU and Allpoint networks, $20.00 for an outgoing domestic wire, $35.00 per Courtesy Pay item, $30.00 for a stop payment, $5.00 for a replacement card, $2.00 for a cashier's check, a 1% international transaction fee on debit purchases, a $5.00 monthly dormant account fee after 12 months of inactivity, and a $25.00 credit card late fee. Balance transfers are free.

Rates change. Check the live page before acting on a number.
