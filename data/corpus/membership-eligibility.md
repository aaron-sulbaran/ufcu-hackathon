Membership at UFCU runs through affiliation. The account opening page lists eligible organizations, and you qualify if you are an employee, student, or alumnus of one of them: Ascension/Seton, Austin Community College, Concordia University Texas, Foundation Communities, Galveston College, Goodwill, Huston-Tillotson University, Indeed, St. Edward's University, Southwestern University, Temple College, Texas A&M at Galveston, the University of Texas at Austin, the University of Texas Medical Branch, UTHealth-Houston, and the YMCA.

If none of those fit, there is a second door. Anyone can join UFCU through the American Consumer Council for free. UFCU handles that as part of enrollment, so it is a checkbox rather than a separate errand.

UFCU's About page states the credit union is confident you are eligible to join and that it serves every individual member without discrimination on grounds including race, color, religion, gender, gender expression, age, national origin, disability, marital status, sexual orientation, military status, or political affiliation.

A Savings account holds your membership share, so membership and a Savings account are opened together. Any other account you want attaches to that membership.
