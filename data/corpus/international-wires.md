Incoming wires to UFCU are free and an outgoing domestic wire costs $20.00.

To receive a wire, the sender uses University Federal Credit Union, Austin, TX, ABA #314977405, with your 13 digit account number, your name, and your address exactly as they appear on the account.

Two things trip up international senders. UFCU does not have an international bank correspondent relationship, so a sending bank abroad must route through its own correspondent bank in the United States and then use UFCU's instructions. For SWIFT messages, the sending bank uses its US correspondent's SWIFT code, which the sender has to get from their own bank. And UFCU cannot receive foreign currency: a wire sent in another currency is converted to US dollars at the US correspondent before it reaches UFCU. Incoming wires post to the checking account by default, or to savings if there is no checking account.

Wire questions go to Member Services at (512) 467-8080 or (800) 252-8311.

For smaller, everyday transfers abroad, UT students commonly use services like Wise or Revolut. Those are not UFCU products and UFCU does not endorse them or cover losses, fees, or exchange rates on them.
