UFCU describes opening an account online as a three to five minute process. The account opening page lists what you bring: a valid driver license or state ID, and a Social Security Number or an ITIN. The page accepts either identifier. You must be 18 or older to open a standard account online. If you do not meet those requirements or do not have those documents, UFCU asks you to schedule an appointment at a branch instead of finishing online, which is how the branch handles cases the web form cannot.

Minimum opening deposits are small. Savings opens with $1 and is the account that establishes membership. Teen Checking, Simply U, Free Checking, and Plus Checking each open with $0. Personal Money Market needs $2,500, a Certificate needs $1,000, and an IRA needs $100 to $1,000.

Under 18, a parent, guardian, or other adult must be a joint owner on the account. That applies to Teen Checking, which serves ages 13 to 17, and to Simply U.

For help or to book an appointment, call (512) 467-8080 or (800) 252-8311, or use appointments.ufcu.org.
