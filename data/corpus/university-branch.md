The University Branch sits at 2244 Guadalupe Street, Austin, TX 78705, on the Drag next to the UT campus, about a five minute walk from most of West Campus. Phone is (512) 467-8080 or (800) 252-8311. The lobby is open Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and closed Saturday.

The branch has enhanced ATMs, including a walk-up ATM that accepts deposits, and an on-site LPL Financial Advisor.

This is the branch that handles the cases the website cannot: documents the online form does not accept, a first account for someone without a US ID history, questions about which account is right, and members who would rather do this with a person. Appointments can be booked at appointments.ufcu.org.

The N Guadalupe Branch at 4611 Guadalupe Street, Austin, TX 78751 is the nearest alternative, open Monday through Friday 9 AM to 5 PM with the drive-thru until 6 PM, and Saturday lobby hours 10 AM to 2 PM.

General Member Services phone hours are Monday through Friday 8 AM to 5:30 PM and Saturday 10 AM to 2 PM.
