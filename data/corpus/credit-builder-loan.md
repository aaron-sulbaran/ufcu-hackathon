The Credit Builder Loan is for members with little credit, no credit, or poor credit. UFCU holds a designated loan amount while you make monthly payments toward it. Each payment is reported to the three major credit bureaus, which is how a credit history gets started from zero. When the payments are finished, you receive the accumulated funds.

Loan amounts run from $500 to $2,500. You choose the term, from 6 to 24 months. The minimum monthly payment is $30. The interest rate is a flat 3.00% APR across every amount and term, and there is no application fee.

The product page says plainly not to worry about having little to no credit, or even poor credit, when you apply. It is subject to credit eligibility and certain restrictions apply.

This is the direct answer to how someone new to the United States, or a student who has never borrowed, builds a credit score without first having one. UFCU branch staff point to it, along with a secured credit card, as the starting point for members who have no credit file at all.
