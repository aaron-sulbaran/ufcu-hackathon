UFCU's forms page is the concrete answer to how you set up direct deposit, and it splits the forms by who pays you.

There is a generic direct deposit form for any employer. There are dedicated forms for Seton Family of Hospitals and for Shriners Burns Hospital. University of Texas employees do not use a paper form at all: the page links to the University of Texas Workday self-service portal, where you enter your UFCU routing and account numbers yourself. UT Medical Branch payroll has its own form. Government payroll and benefits, including Social Security, route through the federal Go Direct portal. UT Student Financial Aid direct deposit has its own electronic funds transfer form.

The same page indexes other forms you may need after opening: deposit and withdrawal slips, wire transfer forms, automatic deposit and payment requests, change of address, and cross-account transfer authorization. Account applications for auto loans, mortgage and home equity, credit cards, and personal loans are indexed here too.

If you work a campus job at UT Austin, the Workday link is the whole path. It takes a few minutes and one paycheck cycle to take effect.
