UFCU offers three Visa credit cards, all with no annual fee, fraud protection, zero liability on unauthorized purchases, contactless payment, and card lock and unlock in the mobile app.

The Great Rate Card is the low-rate option, with rates as low as 9.49% APR and no balance transfer fee. The Cash Back Card earns 1.5% cash back per $1 spent with rates as low as 14.90% APR. The Travel and Rewards Card earns 2 points per $1 spent with rates as low as 13.90% APR.

Eligibility for the Great Rate and Cash Back cards asks you to maintain a UFCU checking account with a $500 or greater balance, a $50 or greater monthly balance for three months, or active direct deposits. All cards are subject to credit approval.

Because UFCU is a federal credit union, the top of its rate range is capped: credit card APRs run from 9.49% to 17.90%, and auto loans top out at 17.90% as well. That cap is a structural difference from a bank card, not a promotion.

UFCU does not publish a separate branded student card.
