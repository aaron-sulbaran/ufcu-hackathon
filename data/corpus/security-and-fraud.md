UFCU separates a scam, a suspicious message or request you have not acted on, from fraud, something that already happened.

The rule that covers most of it: UFCU will never ask for your password, PIN, or a one-time passcode by phone, text, or email. Anyone asking for those is not UFCU. A request to move your money to a safe account is a common scam script, not a bank procedure. When a message is urgent or unexpected, pause and verify it independently, by calling a number you looked up yourself, rather than replying.

For unauthorized card or account activity, call (866) 928-5450 to start a dispute or fraud claim. To report a scam or suspicious activity generally, email fraud@ufcu.org or call (512) 467-8080 and take option 5, then option 4, then option 2.

Peer-to-peer transfers such as Zelle may be treated like cash and are not always reversible, so send only to people you have verified yourself.

Contactless tap-to-pay generates a one-time code per transaction, so the card number is not transmitted and skimmers cannot capture it.
