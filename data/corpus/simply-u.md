Simply U is UFCU's entry-level checking account, built on a spend-what-you-deposit model so overdrafts cannot happen. It costs $0 to open with no minimum opening deposit, and there is no minimum or maximum balance requirement.

There is no monthly service fee, no overdraft fee, and no deposit fee. Bill pay is free. Because the account has no Courtesy Pay, it cannot go into an overdraft you pay for, though the balance can dip temporarily if a merchant's final charge exceeds the authorization hold.

The account comes with an instant-issue contactless Visa debit card. It does not support check writing. The ATM cash withdrawal limit is $1,000 per day and the daily purchase limit is $2,000. The card works internationally with a 1% international service fee.

Standard features include online banking, the mobile app, mobile check deposit, account alerts, and direct deposit setup. Visa Zero Liability covers unauthorized card use.

UFCU frames Simply U as the account for any member who wants the basics without the worry of overspending or monthly fees. A parent or guardian must be on the account for anyone under 18.
