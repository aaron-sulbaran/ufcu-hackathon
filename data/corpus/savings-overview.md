The Savings account is where a UFCU relationship starts. It opens with $1, has no monthly fee, earns monthly dividends up to 0.01% APY, needs a $100 balance to accrue dividends, allows unlimited free transactions, and pays dividends quarterly. This is the account that holds your membership, so it is opened alongside whatever else you want.

Personal Money Market opens with $2,500, has no monthly fee, earns monthly dividends up to 3.25% APY, and lets you deposit and withdraw anytime. It accrues dividends on a $1 balance and pays them monthly.

Certificates open with $1,000, have no monthly fee, and earn up to 4.10% APY. Terms run 3 to 60 months. Early withdrawal penalties apply.

IRAs open with $100 to $1,000, earn up to 4.10% APY, and run 6 to 60 month terms or withdraw anytime. IRS early withdrawal penalties apply.

If you are new to US banking and expecting one account to do everything: checking is for spending and a debit card, savings holds money and earns dividends, and a money market is a savings account with a higher rate and a higher minimum.
