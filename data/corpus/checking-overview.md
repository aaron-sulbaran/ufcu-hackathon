UFCU has four checking accounts and all four open with $0.

Free Checking has no monthly fee and includes Courtesy Pay up to $400. Checks are optional and carry a fee. Daily debit purchase limit is $3,000 and the ATM cash limit is $2,000.

Plus Checking carries a $10 monthly fee that is waived with $10,000 in combined month-end balances or $4,000 in monthly qualifying electronic deposits. In exchange it earns bonus dividends up to 2.25% APY on balances up to $10,000, includes Courtesy Pay up to $1,000, free checks, free money orders and cashier's checks, reimbursed international transaction fees, a 0.25% auto loan rate discount, and a $200 mortgage fee credit. Daily debit purchase limit is $7,500.

Simply U has no monthly fee, no Courtesy Pay, and no check writing.

Teen Checking serves ages 13 to 17, has no monthly fee, earns dividends on balances under $500, includes Courtesy Pay up to $400, and requires a parent, guardian, or other adult as joint owner. It converts to Free Checking at 18. The ATM cash limit is $500 per day.

Every account includes a contactless instant-issue Visa debit card, mobile deposits, and account alerts.
