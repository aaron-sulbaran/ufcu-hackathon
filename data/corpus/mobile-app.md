The UFCU Mobile app runs on iOS 16.0 or newer and Android 12.0 or newer, from the App Store or Google Play. First-time users enroll with their account number plus identifying information. One password works across both the app and online banking. Password rules are 8 to 40 characters with upper and lower case letters and at least one number, and it cannot contain your name or consecutive characters.

Live features include mobile check deposit, bill pay, transfers to UFCU members and to non-members, card management such as activation, locking, and PIN changes, account alerts, transaction history, credit score monitoring, statements, mortgage payments, and credit card cash advances and spend limits. Personal finance management, expense tracking, and savings goals are listed as coming next.

One-time passcodes are valid for 10 minutes and are single use. You can mark a device trusted for 30 days. Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity with a 60 second warning. About one year of transaction history is viewable, and up to 84 months of statements are retrievable.

Maintenance windows run 10 PM to 1 AM daily and midnight to 4 AM Sunday, when some features may be delayed.
