UFCU partners with Zelle for person-to-person payments, and Zelle appears alongside PayPal and the digital wallets in UFCU's payment services. The Zelle page itself is thin: it does not publish transaction limits, fees, or enrollment steps, so detail questions go to Member Services at (512) 467-8080 or (800) 252-8311, or to an appointment at appointments.ufcu.org.

One thing the website does not say, and members ask about constantly: Zelle enrollment requires a Social Security Number. Per UFCU branch staff, that means international students without an SSN, business accounts, and organizational accounts do not get Zelle. If you have no SSN yet, plan around it rather than being surprised at enrollment.

Alternatives inside digital banking: Transfer to a Non-UFCU Member sends funds to any US email address or phone number with no UFCU fee, and UFCU to UFCU transfers are instant. For money crossing a border, see the wire transfer instructions.

UFCU's security FAQ warns that peer-to-peer transfers like Zelle may be treated like cash and are not always reversible. Send only to people you have verified yourself.
